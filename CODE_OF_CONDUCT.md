# Code of Conduct

GraphGPT contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to create a welcoming,
professional, and harassment-free environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive in reviews and discussions.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical and social impact directly.
- Welcome people of different backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, private reports, or personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, and unwanted sexual attention
are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Maintainers
will review reports confidentially where possible and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
remove participants based on severity and repeated behavior. Retaliation against reporters is not
tolerated.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version
2.1.
